  - Hardware → Screens → “Put lyrics on your live stream” is now a full editor for all three looks: Over your camera, Lower third, and Full projector look.
  - The preview shows the slide that is ACTUALLY live right now, exactly as OBS will show it (or a sample song/verse when nothing is live).
  - Over your camera (Full projector layout): text size, top/middle/bottom position, text colour, shadow or outline, and a dark background behind the words. Lower third: band style, height, position, text size, see-through, text colour and show/hide the verse reference. Full projector look: text size and darken background.
  - Every tweak reaches OBS instantly while the app is open. Changing the LOOK itself only reaches links you create or copy from this new editor — links you already added to OBS keep their look, so nothing changes on your stream by surprise.
  - Your own lower-third title (name / title lines) now shows on your OBS stream: in the band for the Lower third look, and on the Full projector look. On Over your camera it shows inside the band in the Lower third layout, or as a caption over the camera in the Full projector layout. It stays up until you send the next slide, or until you clear it.
  - Your projector and operator screen are unchanged.
