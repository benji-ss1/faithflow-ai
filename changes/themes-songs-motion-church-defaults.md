---
headline: Songs follow your theme, new animated backgrounds, and one place for your church defaults
audience: operator
version: 0.1.521
date: 2026-09-23
highlights:
  - Songs now follow the church theme the same way Bible verses do. A song you styled yourself keeps your own look.
  - New animated backgrounds in the Themes library, and fixes for flicker and glitches when you switch animated backgrounds.
  - New "Church defaults" in Settings (and on the Organization page). An admin picks the default Bible translation, the main theme and the default animated background once, and every service starts with them.
  - Applying a theme during a service now changes the screens for that service only. The main theme (marked with a star) is what loads every time the app starts. Theme cards show "Main theme" and "Live now" so you can tell which is which.
  - To change the main theme, use "Set as main" on a theme. In the Bible panel, admins can use "Make default" to make the current translation the church default. Switching translation during a service, including by voice, still only lasts for that service.
  - If the app can't reach the server, it now uses your church's saved default translation instead of falling back to KJV.
  - New churches can pick their translation, main theme and animated background during setup, or skip this step.
