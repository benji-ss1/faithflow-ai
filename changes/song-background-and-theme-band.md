---
headline: A Background button on song slides, and themes can now use the third band
audience: operator
version: 0.1.490
date: 2026-09-20
highlights:
  - Song slides now have a Background button on the slide itself. You could always set a background per slide by dragging an image from the Media bin onto it, or by right-clicking, but nothing showed you that, so it looked like the whole theme had to be changed.
  - The Background menu now has Choose image, so you can pick a picture from your media library instead of having to drag one in first. It applies to that one slide, shows straight away, and goes to the projector if that slide is live.
  - Themes can now use the Third band for scripture. In the theme editor there is a new Scripture layout choice of Full screen or Third band, so a church can get a band from its theme without having to save a church-wide Scripture Style first.
  - A saved Scripture Style still overrides the theme, exactly as before, and a theme that says nothing about scripture is unchanged.
---
