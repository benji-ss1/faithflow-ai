---
headline: "Next verse" now works when it follows a sentence, and "do we have verse 7" works at all
audience: operator
version: 0.1.488
date: 2026-09-20
highlights:
  - Saying a sentence, then a full stop or a comma, then the command now works. "And that is why he came. Next verse." and "That's the whole point, next verse please" used to be ignored for being too long.
  - When one sentence contains two commands, the last one wins. "Go back to verse 4. Do we have verse 7, please?" now goes to verse 7. It used to go nowhere at all.
  - Asking for a numbered verse in the natural way now works - "do we have verse 7", "do you have verse 7 please", "show me verse 9", "can we see verse 3", "read verse 11", "turn to verse 6", "pull up verse 2", "bring up verse 5", "take us to verse 4". None of these were recognised before.
  - Ordinary preaching is still ignored. "In the next verse, Paul says...", "let us go back to the beginning" and "I want us to see. Continue reading" do not move the screen.
