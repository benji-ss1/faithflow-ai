---
headline: New "Audio interface & Blackmagic" panel in Audio
audience: operator
version: 0.1.443
date: 2026-09-17
highlights:
  - Audio now has an "Audio interface & Blackmagic" panel next to NDI network audio. It finds your USB mixer or interface (X32, XR18, SQ, WING, Focusrite and more) and Blackmagic inputs, and shows how many channels each has.
  - Click a device to use it. On Windows, PresentFlow switches on the pro audio driver for you and picks the version that shows every channel.
  - It rescans when you plug a device in or out, and there's a Rescan button. "Stop interface" goes back to your computer's default input.
  - If the pro audio driver ever has a problem on a computer, PresentFlow now falls back to standard capture instead of closing.
