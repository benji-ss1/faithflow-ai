---
headline: Clearing the Slide now leaves your theme's background on screen
audience: operator
version: 0.1.479
date: 2026-09-19
highlights:
  - When you clear the Slide (the Slide button on the clear rail, F2, or the Slide row in the Layers panel), the words go but the theme's background picture, video or animation stays on the projector, the way ProPresenter does it.
  - The Media button now lights up while that theme background is showing. Press it, F3, or the Media row to take the background off, or use Clear All to clear everything.
  - Nothing changes when nothing has been shown yet (the screen stays black), when a slide has its own background picture, or on the see-through OBS and NDI outputs.
  - Escape, the X on the live preview and the kill switch still black the whole screen out, as before.
