headline: Over your camera: choose Lower third or Full projector
audience: operator
version: 0.1.403
date: 2026-09-14
order: 2
highlights:
  - Over your camera now has a Layout choice: Lower third (a see-through band over your camera — the default once you open the OBS editor on the computer running your service) or Full projector (the words across the whole camera picture, as before). In Lower third you can put the band Upper, Mid, Lower or a Custom position, and set its background, height, text size, see-through and text colour.
  - In the camera Lower third layout, announcements, messages and timers are hidden so only the words sit over your camera. Switch the Layout to Full projector to show them.
